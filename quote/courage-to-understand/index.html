The Courage to Understand

You already know enough. So do I. It is not knowledge we lack. What is missing is the courage to understand what we know and to draw conclusions.

— Sven Lindqvist

When I read this quote by Sven Lindqvist in his book Exterminate All the Brutes, I was shaken so much I put the book down. I knew that I was responsible to act on this information. When faced with an injustice, it is not enough to acknowledge that something is not fair. I realized that true belief requires action even when what must be done is uncomfortable.

“You already know enough. So do I. It is not knowledge we lack. What is missing is the courage to understand what we know and to draw conclusions.”
